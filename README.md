# internet-website
the internet of all recursive recursively

## Abyfaultadefault · Temaline Final Edition

Open `index.html` in a browser. The homepage runs without dependencies or a build step.

- **Flood Fever** is the three-circuit Femae puzzle from [Temaline](https://github.com/necurookami2026-jpg/temaline-website). Rotate conduits with a click or tap, or use the arrow keys to move and Enter or Space to turn.
- **History** contains the Historical Internet of All Recursive. Open the eras and milestones to follow the timeline and visit its sources. The timeline works offline. External sources require a connection.
